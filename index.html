Our story

Brewed with care, served with heart.

Ember & Oat started with a tiny roaster and a big idea: that the best café is the one that feels like your own kitchen, only with better coffee.

We source beans directly from growers, bake everything on site, and work with local farms for our eggs, dairy and produce.
